# Final Manual Playtest

Run `BUILD_WINDOWS.bat`, then complete this checklist before submitting:

1. Intro shows the emblem/background, plays music, and reaches the menu.
2. Menu buttons play a click sound and both Start/Exit respond correctly.
3. Place exactly four allies and begin the game.
4. Verify Damian shoots arrows, Junior fires purple magic bolts, and
   Evangeline/Lucas use melee attacks.
5. Verify the new hero and monster PNGs display without opaque backgrounds.
6. Play waves 1–4 and confirm planned counts 1, 4, 6, and 7 (Wave 4 includes the Boss).
7. Buy each upgrade once and confirm its SFX and HUD level/cost update; use
   `Backspace` to undo the latest purchase and restore its gold/stat.
8. Press `P`; entities, projectiles, wave timers, boss beam, and music pause.
9. At Boss 50% HP, confirm yellow warning rays precede red damaging rays.
10. Stand on one active ray: the Player loses 22 HP at most once per volley; allies
    do not take beam damage.
11. When Boss crosses 25% HP, confirm the Enraged notice appears once and the Boss becomes faster but does not accelerate
    every frame.
12. Finish the Boss and remaining enemies; the Win screen and sound appear.
13. Restart and intentionally lose; the Game Over screen and sound appear.
